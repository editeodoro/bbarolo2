# PGC 53639: a warped disk seen nearly face-on

The HI of PGC 53639, from the Local Volume HI Survey with the Australia Telescope Compact Array, is a disk at
low inclination with a strong warp: the kinematic major axis turns by almost 200° from the centre to the edge,
and the velocity field is S-shaped. The example fits it with {doc}`3DFIT <../tasks/3dfit/index>` (METHOD CLASSIC)
with the position angle free in each ring, and the inclination fixed.

## The data

| | |
|---|---|
| Object | PGC 53639, a nearby gas-rich galaxy, systemic velocity 589 km/s |
| Data | HI 21-cm line, ATCA, Local Volume HI Survey (LVHIS; Koribalski et al. 2018) |
| Cube | 125 × 119 pixels of 10 arcsec (binned 2 × 2 by BBarolo), 58 channels of 4 km/s |
| Beam | 63.0 × 51.7 arcsec |
| Files | [pgc53639.fits](https://github.com/editeodoro/bbarolo2/raw/main/examples/pgc53639.fits) (3.5 MB), [pgc53639_3DFIT.par](https://github.com/editeodoro/bbarolo2/raw/main/examples/pgc53639_3DFIT.par) |

:::

:::{tab-item} Python
:sync: py

```python
from bbarolo import FitMod3D

f = FitMod3D("pgc53639.fits", specres=0.425, nradii=16, radsep=30, inc=25, pa=150, vsys=589, xpos=61.9,
             ypos=63.1, free="VROT VDISP PA", mask="SEARCH", minvdisp=5, deltapa=50, flagerrors=True)
res = f.run(threads=16)     # in memory
res.rings                   # the rings of the second stage, with the errors
res.write()                 # the files of the command line, in output/PGC53639/
```
:::
::::

On our server, with 16 cores, the run takes **24 seconds**.

## The setup

- **Spectral resolution.** {ref}`SPECRES <par-3dfit-specres>` 0.425 channels (1/2.355, also the default) is a
  Gaussian line spread function with a FWHM of one channel (see {doc}`../user-guide/spectral-resolution`).
- **Rings.** 16 rings of 30 arcsec, about half the beam, out to 480 arcsec.
- **Inclination.** At low inclination the rotation velocity and the inclination are degenerate (the velocities
  along the line of sight scale with $V_\mathrm{rot} \sin i$), and the shape of the rings tells little about the
  inclination. INC is therefore fixed at 25°. The rotation velocities scale with $1/\sin i$: an inclination
  of 20° or 30° would change them by +24% or −15%.
- **Position angle.** PA is free ({ref}`FREE <par-3dfit-free>` `VROT VDISP PA`) and, because it changes by much
  more than the default ±15°, it can move by ±50° ({ref}`DELTAPA <par-3dfit-deltapa>`). The initial value, 150°, is
  only a start: when PA is free, a 2D fit of the velocity field on the rings of 3DFIT first refines the initial PA of
  each ring (`_2drings.txt`; see {doc}`../tasks/3dfit/algorithm`), and the 3D fit then moves it within ±50° of that.
  In the second stage the PA is regularised by a smooth (Bézier) function of radius, chosen automatically
  ({ref}`REGTYPE <par-3dfit-regtype>`).
- **Initial values.** The centre and the systemic velocity are given; VROT is estimated from the data (29 km/s) and
  VDISP starts at the default, 8 km/s. {ref}`MINVDISP <par-3dfit-minvdisp>` lets the dispersion go down to 5 km/s.
- **Mask and errors.** The mask is that of the source finder ({ref}`MASK <par-3dfit-mask>` `SEARCH`);
  {ref}`FLAGERRORS <par-3dfit-flagerrors>` estimates the errors.

## Results

The position angle turns from 268° in the centre to 78° in the last ring, smoothly, by about 190° over 450 arcsec.
The rotation curve rises from 28 km/s to 80 km/s at 225 arcsec and stays between 75 and 90 km/s further out, with
errors growing to 20 km/s in the outer rings; the dispersion falls from 12 to 5 km/s.

structures of the gas that an axisymmetric model does not have.
