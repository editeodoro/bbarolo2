# UGC 1281: a global fit of an edge-on dwarf

UGC 1281 is a dwarf galaxy seen almost edge-on. In an edge-on disk every line of sight crosses many rings, so the
rings cannot be fitted one at a time: the whole disk must be fitted at once. This example does it with
{doc}`3DFIT <../tasks/3dfit/index>` and `METHOD GLOBAL`, the maximum of the likelihood of all the parameters
together: the rotation velocity and the dispersion of each ring, the geometry and the thickness of the disk, its
outer edge and an error of the model, with smoothness priors on the profiles and errors from the Laplace
approximation.

## The data

| | |
|---|---|
| Object | UGC 1281, a dwarf galaxy seen edge-on, systemic velocity 157 km/s |
| Data | HI 21-cm line, Westerbork Synthesis Radio Telescope, WHISP survey (van der Hulst et al. 2001; Swaters et al. 2002), at a resolution of 30 arcsec |
| Cube | 52 × 57 pixels of 10 arcsec, 57 channels of 4.12 km/s |
| Beam | 30 × 30 arcsec |
| Files | [ugc1281.fits](https://github.com/editeodoro/bbarolo2/raw/main/examples/ugc1281.fits) (0.7 MB), [ugc1281_3DFIT_GLOBAL.par](https://github.com/editeodoro/bbarolo2/raw/main/examples/ugc1281_3DFIT_GLOBAL.par) |

```bash

```
:::

:::{tab-item} Python
:sync: py

```python
from bbarolo import FitMod3D

f = FitMod3D("ugc1281.fits", method="global", specres=0.425, nradii=8, radsep=30, inc=85,
             free="vrot vdisp inc_single phi_single z0_single xpos_single ypos_single vsys_single radmax fracerr",
             mask="SEARCH", flagerrors=True, likeopts="smooth=(vrot=2.3,vdisp=1)")
res = f.run(threads=16)     # in memory
res.rings                   # the best fit, with the errors
res.fitter.print_stats()    # the free parameters, with the errors
res.fitter.corner_plot()    # the corner plot (a matplotlib Figure)
res.write()                 # the files of the command line, in output/UGC1281/
```
:::
::::

On our server, with 16 cores, the run takes **38 seconds**: 9 seconds for the fit (4426 likelihoods) and most of
the rest for the errors, which need the Hessian of the log-likelihood. Without FLAGERRORS it takes 15 seconds.

## The setup

- **Rings.** 8 rings of 30 arcsec, one beam, out to 240 arcsec. Only the number and the width of the rings and the
  inclination are given: the centre, the systemic velocity, the position angle and the rotation velocity are
  estimated from the data (in the log: XPOS 25.3, YPOS 27.7 pixels, VSYS 155.9 km/s, PA 221°, VROT 61 km/s), and
  are the starting point of the fit.
- **Free parameters.** VROT and VDISP in each ring (`vrot vdisp`); one inclination, position angle, scale height,
  centre and systemic velocity for the whole disk (`_single`); the outer edge of the disk (`radmax`), and a
  fractional error of the model (`fracerr`): 24 parameters. See {ref}`FREE <par-3dfit-free>`; in Python and in
  the outputs the position angle is called `phi`.
- **Priors.** The default ones ({ref}`PRIORS <par-3dfit-priors>`), which follow the initial values: INC between
  75° and 90°, PA ±15° and the centre ±2 beams around the estimates, VSYS ±5 channels, VROT from 0 to twice the
  estimate, VDISP from 0 to 50 km/s, Z0 from 0 to one beam, RADMAX from one beam inside the last radius to the
  outer edge of the last ring (195 to 240 arcsec), and FRACERR log-uniform between 0.001 and 1. The log of the run
  lists them.
- **Thickness.** In an edge-on disk the scale height Z0 is measured directly, from the extent of the emission
  perpendicular to the plane, so it is free here.
- **RADMAX.** The outer edge of the gas: the density of the model is cut at RADMAX, which need not be at the end of
  the last ring (see {doc}`../tasks/3dfit/likelihood`).
- **FRACERR.** The model is not a perfect description of the data: FRACERR adds to the noise of each voxel an error
  proportional to the model, so that the misfit does not shrink the errors of the parameters.
- **Smoothness.** Along an edge-on line of sight the rings overlap, and the profiles of VROT and VDISP can zigzag
  from ring to ring with little change of the likelihood. The smoothness priors of
  {ref}`LIKEOPTS <par-3dfit-likeopts>` `smooth=(vrot=2.3,vdisp=1)` penalise the bending of the profiles: VROT may
  bend by about 2.3 km/s and VDISP by 1 km/s over one beam.
- **Errors.** With METHOD GLOBAL, {ref}`FLAGERRORS <par-3dfit-flagerrors>` computes the errors of the Laplace
  approximation (the inverse of the Hessian at the best fit), and 4000 draws of that Gaussian stand in for the
  posterior samples, for the corner plot and the error columns. The draws are random, so the errors change by a few
  per cent from run to run (the best fit does not).
- **Spectral resolution.** {ref}`SPECRES <par-3dfit-specres>` 0.425 channels is a line spread function with a FWHM
  of one channel.

## Results

| Parameter | Best fit |
|---|---|
| INC | 87.8° ± 0.9° |
| PA | 217.0° ± 0.2° |
| Z0 | 10.8 ± 0.6 arcsec |
| VSYS | 157.2 ± 0.4 km/s |
| RADMAX | 228 (−10, +9) arcsec |
| FRACERR | 0.12 ± 0.01 |

The rotation curve rises nearly linearly to 59 km/s at 135 arcsec and is flat beyond, with errors of 2 km/s
(6 km/s in the last ring); the dispersion is between 6 and 13 km/s. The reduced χ² of the best fit is 1.25 (1.06
with the model error). The log warns that VROT of the first ring and VDISP of the last are within their errors of a
bound of the prior (VROT = 3 km/s, VDISP = 7.5 ± 6 km/s): their errors are less reliable. It also warns that
the Hessian is not positive definite in some direction of the parameters: the Laplace approximation is only an
approximation here, and METHOD BAYESIAN would sample the real shape of the posterior.
