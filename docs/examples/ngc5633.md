# NGC 5633: Hα and [NII] in an optical IFU cube

An integral-field cube of the spiral galaxy NGC 5633 from the CALIFA survey, in the low-resolution setup V500.
The cube has the stellar continuum and three emission lines, Hα and the [NII] doublet, in channels of 2 Å
(90 km/s). The example shows how to fit such data with {doc}`3DFIT <../tasks/3dfit/index>`: the continuum is
subtracted first ({doc}`CONTSUB <../tasks/contsub/index>`), the three lines are modelled together with fixed flux
ratios, and the beam and the spectral resolution, which the header does not give, are set by hand.

## The data

| | |
|---|---|
| Object | NGC 5633, a spiral galaxy at z = 0.0076 |
| Data | optical IFU, PPak at the Calar Alto 3.5-m telescope, CALIFA survey, setup V500 (Sánchez et al. 2012) |
| Cube | 77 × 72 spaxels of 1 arcsec, 100 channels of 2 Å from 6515 to 6713 Å |
| Resolution | point spread function 1.7 arcsec (`BEAM`), line spread function of 1.2 channels (`SPECRES`, dispersion) |
| Files | [NGC5633_V500.fits](https://github.com/editeodoro/bbarolo2/raw/main/examples/NGC5633_V500.fits) (2.3 MB), [NGC5633_3DFIT.par](https://github.com/editeodoro/bbarolo2/raw/main/examples/NGC5633_3DFIT.par) |

:::{tab-item} Python
:sync: py

```python
from bbarolo import FitMod3D

f = FitMod3D("NGC5633_V500.fits", contsub=True, exclwind="37:68", contorder=1,
             beam=1.7, specres=1.2, veldef="OPTICAL",
             restwave=[6564.61, 6549.86, 6585.27], relint=[1, 0.15, 0.46], redshift=0.00757,
             nradii=17, radsep=1.7, xpos=35.0, ypos=32.6, inc=50, pa=199, vrot=150, vdisp=25, vsys=-33, z0=1,
             mask="SEARCH", threshold=0.2, growththreshold=0.03, threshvelocity=5,
             free="VROT VDISP", norm="AZIM", minvdisp=15, flagerrors=True, maptype="GAUSSIAN")
res = f.run(threads=16)     # the continuum subtraction, then the fit, in memory
res.rings                   # the fitted rings, with the errors
res.write()                 # the files of the command line, in output/NGC5633/
```
:::
::::

On our server, with 16 cores, the run takes **25 seconds**.

## The setup

- **Continuum.** {ref}`CONTSUB <par-contsub-contsub>` fits a polynomial of degree 1
  ({ref}`CONTORDER <par-contsub-contorder>`) to the spectrum of each spaxel and subtracts it, leaving out the channels
  37 to 68, where the lines are ({ref}`EXCLWIND <par-contsub-exclwind>`). The continuum-subtracted cube is
  written in `_contsub.fits` and is the one fitted.
- **Resolution.** The header has no beam: {ref}`BEAM <par-all-beam>` gives the FWHM of the point spread function,
  1.7 arcsec. {ref}`SPECRES <par-3dfit-specres>` is the dispersion of the line spread function in channels, 1.2
  (2.4 Å, about 110 km/s at Hα): the instrumental broadening is much larger than the velocity dispersion of the
  gas, which is therefore poorly constrained.
- **Lines.** The spectral axis is in wavelength: the velocities are computed from the rest wavelength of the first
  line of {ref}`RESTWAVE <par-galmod-restwave>` (Hα, in vacuum) at {ref}`REDSHIFT <par-galmod-redshift>`, with the
  optical definition ({ref}`VELDEF <par-all-veldef>`). The model has the three lines at the same velocities, with
  the intensities of {ref}`RELINT <par-galmod-relint>` relative to Hα: 0.15 for [NII]6548 and 0.46 for [NII]6583.
  VSYS is the velocity relative to REDSHIFT.
- **Rings and geometry.** 17 rings of 1.7 arcsec, one beam. The centre, VSYS, INC (50°) and PA (199°) are given
  and fixed: only VROT and VDISP are free.
- **Mask.** {ref}`MASK <par-3dfit-mask>` `SEARCH` with thresholds in flux units
  ({ref}`THRESHOLD <par-search-threshold>` 0.2 and {ref}`GROWTHTHRESHOLD <par-search-growththreshold>` 0.03, in the
  units of the cube, which has no BUNIT) rather than in signal-to-noise ratio; detections closer than 5 channels
  are merged ({ref}`THRESHVELOCITY <par-search-threshvelocity>`).
- **Dispersion.** {ref}`MINVDISP <par-3dfit-minvdisp>` 15 km/s is the lowest VDISP allowed: with an instrumental
  dispersion of 110 km/s, smaller values cannot be measured.
- **Maps.** {ref}`MAPTYPE <par-maps-maptype>` `GAUSSIAN` makes the velocity and dispersion maps of the plots from
  Gaussian fits of the spectra rather than from moments, which are noisy in channels this wide.

## Results

The rotation curve rises to 150 km/s at 10 arcsec and is between 150 and 180 km/s beyond, with errors of 10 to
50 km/s. The dispersion is between 15 and 42 km/s with errors of about 20 km/s: within the errors, it is not
measured, as expected from the spectral resolution.

The model reproduces the three lines along both axes, with the right ratios. The emission of the data is
clumpy (star-forming regions along the spiral arms), and the model, an axisymmetric disk, is smooth: the
clumps are the largest residuals of the intensity map. The observed dispersion (100–130 km/s) is
mostly the instrumental broadening.
